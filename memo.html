Stop 2 of 6Plug two leaks

Two revenue leaks I found as a paying user.

Both cost revenue quietly. Both are fast, high-ROI fixes. Toggle each card to see the solution.

Leak 01 Referral Abuse Leaking RevenueFixed

Twelve burner email addresses get anyone a year of Pro, free.

Pro months unlocked through my own test referrals

I referred secondary addresses I own. Each gave a free month of Pro, and two people can dictate simultaneously on one account. I tested this myself.

Enforce one referral reward per hardware device. Second claim prompts: "This device has already redeemed a referral month."

Revenue Leaked$144of Pro per user who games it
Fix Effort1 weekbusiness rules, not a rebuild
  • One referral reward per verified hardware device ID.
  • Reward the referrer only after the friend completes active dictations, not on signup.
  • One live dictation session per account to prevent plan-sharing.
Leak 02 Payments in India Leaking RevenueFixed

My UPI Autopay failed twice, and Pro quietly lapsed.

What happened to my subscription

Both times the mandate failed, nothing was charged, and no notification was sent. A paying user was dropped without either side intending it.

When a mandate fails, Flow retries and sends a 1-tap WhatsApp payment link while Pro continues running during a grace period.

Revenue Leaked₹3,840annual loss per dropped subscriber
Fix Effort2 weekssmart retries, grace period, WhatsApp link
  • Automated mandate retry schedule + instant 1-tap WhatsApp pay link.
  • 7-day grace period so power users aren't interrupted while payments resolve.
  • Track mandate health as a distinct KPI in the conversion funnel.

The referral leak may be intentional for early land-grab, but uncontrolled cannibalization hurts ARR. It must be instrumented, not guessed.

  1. 1
    First dictation in WhatsApp, in Hinglish. India is mobile-first and lives in messaging apps. Activate users where they already communicate every hour. Model: +12 pts India activation
  2. 2
    Fix Autopay, then make annual the default. 75% of Indian buyers already select annual plans. Once mandates stick reliably, make annual the default 1-tap choice. Model: +3 pts India paid conversion

Willingness to pay outside Tier-1 cities should be tested rigorously against a holdout region before rolling out nationwide.
