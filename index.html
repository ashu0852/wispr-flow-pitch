Stop 2 of 6Plug two leaks

Two leaks I found as a paying user.

Both cost revenue quietly. Both are small fixes. Flip each card to see the fix.

Leak 1ReferralsLeakingFixed

Twelve email addresses get anyone a year of Pro, free.

Pro months unlocked through my own referrals

I referred addresses I own. Each one gave a free month of Pro, and two people can dictate on one account at once. I tested it on myself.

One reward per device. The second claim says "This device has already used a referral month."

What it costs$144of Pro, free, per person who games it
Fix effort1 weekrules, not a rebuild
  • One referral reward per device.
  • Pay the referrer after the friend really dictates, not at sign-up.
  • One live session per account, so a plan can't be shared.
Leak 2Payments in IndiaLeakingFixed

My UPI Autopay failed twice, and Pro quietly lapsed.

What happened to my payment

Both times the mandate failed, nothing was charged, and nobody told me. A paying user lost without either side deciding it.

The mandate fails, Flow retries and sends a one-tap pay link on WhatsApp, and Pro keeps running in the meantime.

What it costs₹3,840a year, for every payer it drops
Fix effort2 weeksretries, grace period, a WhatsApp link
  • Retry the mandate, plus a one-tap WhatsApp pay link before Pro ends.
  • A short grace period, so Pro runs while payment retries.
  • Track mandate success as its own step in the paid funnel.

The referral leak might be deliberate, since free months that turn into habits are cheap acquisition. Then it's worth measuring, not guessing.
