Funnel Leak 01 · Referral Cannibalization

$1.0M+ARR Leaking

Unlimited burner emails get free Pro indefinitely, and accounts are shared.

A single user can cycle throwaway emails to avoid paying $144/year while dictating simultaneously on multiple devices.

Exploit: Unlimited free Pro months 1-Week Fix: 1 reward per hardware device ID
The Leak Math: Across 2.5M+ downloads, if just 1.5% of active users cycle burner emails or share seats, Flow bleeds over $1.0M in cannibalized Pro upgrades.

The 1-Week Fix:
• Lock referral rewards to verified hardware device IDs (second claim displays: "Device already redeemed").
• Reward referrer only after friend completes 5 active dictations, not on sign-up.
• Enforce 1 active live dictation session per account to stop plan-sharing.
